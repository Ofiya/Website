Our Sacred History

Celestial Church of Christ, Redemption Parish, is a faith-based non-profit established in 2016, serving Winnipeg as a spiritual haven for racialized minorities through worship, counselling, mentorship, and outreach. Guided by Christ's teachings, we embrace faith as a force for personal and community transformation.

Our Celestial Vision

We are committed to creating a vibrant and supportive environment focused on Jesus Christ. Our goal is to empower individuals and build an inclusive community where faith serves as the foundation for transformation, dedicated service, and ongoing personal growth.

  • Empower marginalized individuals & families — providing spiritual nourishment tailored to their specific challenges and goals.
  • Strengthen social bonds — fostering fellowship, mutual support, and encouragement.
  • Economic initiatives — helping members thrive and achieve financial independence.
  • A symbol of hope and excellence — equipping individuals with faith-based teachings, practical skills, and unwavering confidence.
  • Love, unity & compassion — influencing every interaction and project; promoting harmony across cultures and generations.

Through this vision, we aim to demonstrate faith-driven leadership and meaningful community involvement.

Our Divine Mandate

The mandate of Celestial Church of Christ, Redemption Parish, is to promote the Christian faith and improve the social and economic well-being of individuals and families in our community. We strive to be a spiritual home and a centre for community development, where faith inspires practical empowerment.

Guided by the teachings of Jesus Christ, our Parish is committed to:

  • Promoting spiritual growth and moral values through worship and education
  • Empowering youth and families with mentorship and skills training
  • Fostering inclusion among people of colour
  • Participating in charitable initiatives to support vulnerable groups
  • Building partnerships to strengthen community capacity

Our ultimate aim is to transform lives through faith-driven service, fostering a community where individuals are spiritually rooted and equipped to contribute to Canadian society.

Our Sacred Core Values

Love & Compassion

We demonstrate God's unconditional love through tangible acts of service, kindness, and genuine care for all, especially the marginalized and vulnerable in our community.

Holiness & Integrity

We strive for spiritual purity and moral excellence in all we do, reflecting Christ's character and upholding the sacred teachings of the Celestial Church of Christ.

Service & Empowerment

We believe faith without works is incomplete. We actively serve, equip, and empower individuals and families to overcome challenges and thrive in every aspect of life.

Inclusive Community

We embrace diversity as a gift from God, fostering an environment where people from all racial, cultural, and generational backgrounds feel seen, valued, and belong.

Transformative Faith

We view faith as a dynamic force for personal and societal change — prayerful, action-oriented, and committed to seeing lives, families, and our city transformed by God's power.

Spiritual Nourishment

We are dedicated to providing rich spiritual resources, worship, teaching, and pastoral care that address the unique challenges and aspirations of every soul we serve.
